YOUR GUIDE

Practical tips for student life

Use these ideas as a starting point, then verify current local prices and availability before making plans.

Explore outdoor spaces

Plan a walk, a picnic, or a casual meetup in a public space. Check current access and hours before visiting.

Look for campus events

University calendars can be a useful starting point for lectures, performances, and student activities.

Plan a low-cost group day

Choose a spending limit ahead of time and look for activities that do not require everyone to buy a ticket.

Check before you go

Event pricing and availability can change. Verify details with the organizer.
