YOUR GUIDE

Practical tips for student life

Use these ideas as a starting point, then verify current local prices and availability before making plans.

Compare parking rules

Check hourly rates, permit restrictions, enforcement times, and maximum stay before choosing a space.

Consider transit and walking

For short trips, compare the time and total cost of public transportation, biking, and walking.

Budget for recurring trips

Estimate how often you commute each week rather than focusing only on the price of one trip.

Verify official details

Use official city, campus, and transit information for current fares, routes, and parking rules.
