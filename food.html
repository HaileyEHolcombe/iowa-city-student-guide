YOUR GUIDE

Practical tips for student life

Use these ideas as a starting point, then verify current local prices and availability before making plans.

Compare meal value

When choosing a meal, compare the total cost after tax and any delivery or service fees. A filling option can offer better value than a smaller discounted item.

Plan groceries around meals

Make a short list of meals you can prepare, then check unit prices and compare store brands before shopping.

Ask about student offers

Some establishments may have time-limited or eligibility-based offers. Confirm any student discount directly with the business before relying on it.

Reduce food waste

Choose ingredients you can use across several meals and plan around what you already have.
